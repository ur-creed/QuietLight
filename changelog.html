1.1

Release February 2026

Reflection rhythm

A few quiet additions to help reflection fit gently into your day — without pressure or prescribed outcomes.

  • Daily reflections Three thoughtfully spaced reflections per day, each with its own prompt and presence.
  • Daily reminders Set a gentle nudge to pause and reflect at a time that suits you.
  • Calm meter See your reflections for the day at a glance, with a soft cue when it's a good moment to pause.
  • A kinder daily rhythm When you've reflected enough for one day, you're met with a calm note that rest is part of the practice.
