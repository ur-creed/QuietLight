Quiet Light

A contemplative AI companion

Quiet Light is a contemplative companion that offers symbolic reflection, gentle guidance, and spacious presence. It moves through gentle language and metaphor, illuminating what may already be here — without claiming truth or authority.

Three presences are available: Quiet Light (clear and minimal), Quiet Sun (radiant and warm), and Quiet Moon (soft and nurturing). Each creates a distinct quality of stillness, returning clarity and agency entirely to you.

It is not a healer, therapist, or authority. Any insight, perspective, or question it offers is an invitation you are free to set down — never a command or verdict. The meaning, and the choosing, remain entirely yours.

Its purpose is to offer light, resonance, or tenderness…

…and then to step back.
