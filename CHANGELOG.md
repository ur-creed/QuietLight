## 1.1 — Reflection rhythm · Release · February 2026

A few quiet additions to help reflection fit gently into your day.

- **Daily reflections** — three thoughtfully spaced reflections per day
- **Daily reminders** — optional gentle nudges to pause and reflect
- **Calm meter** — reflections for the day at a glance
- **A kinder daily rhythm** — rest is part of the practice

## 1.0 — First light · Release · January 2026

Initial App Store release.

- **Three presences** — Quiet Light, Quiet Sun, and Quiet Moon
- **Gentle companionship** — reflection without pressure or verdicts
- **Free tier** — three daily reflections, always complete
- **Premium** — deeper companionship, continuity, and richer responses
